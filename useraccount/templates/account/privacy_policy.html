Introduction

Welcome to MediTrack+. We are committed to protecting your privacy and the security of your health information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our healthcare management platform.

HIPAA Compliance: MediTrack+ operates as a HIPAA-compliant entity. We are committed to maintaining the privacy and security of your Protected Health Information (PHI) as required by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act and other applicable laws.

By using MediTrack+, you consent to the data practices described in this policy. If you do not agree with the terms of this Privacy Policy, please do not access or use the Service.

Information We Collect

We collect several types of information to provide and improve our Service to you:

📝 Personal Information

  • Name, email address, phone number
  • Date of birth and gender
  • Contact information
  • Account credentials

🏥 Protected Health Information (PHI)

  • Medical history and records
  • Medication information
  • Treatment plans
  • Lab results and test reports
  • Insurance information
  • Billing and payment information

📊 Usage & Technical Data

  • IP address and device information
  • Browser type and version
  • Pages visited and time spent
  • App features used
  • Error logs and performance data

2.1 How We Collect Information

We collect information through:

  • Direct Collection: Information you provide when creating an account, updating your profile, or entering health data
  • Automatic Collection: Usage data collected through cookies and similar technologies
  • Third-Party Sources: Information from healthcare providers, laboratories, or insurance companies with your authorization
  • Health Devices: Data from connected health devices (with your permission)

For users in the European Union, we process your personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: When you have given clear consent
  • Contract: To fulfill our contractual obligations
  • Legal Obligation: To comply with legal requirements
  • Vital Interests: To protect vital interests
  • Legitimate Interests: For our legitimate business interests

How We Protect Your Information

🔐 Enterprise-Grade Security: We implement comprehensive security measures to protect your health information.

4.1 Security Measures

Our security measures include:

  • End-to-End Encryption: All health data is encrypted in transit and at rest
  • Access Controls: Role-based access controls and authentication
  • Regular Audits: Security audits and penetration testing
  • Data Backup: Regular encrypted backups
  • Physical Security: Secure data centers
  • Employee Training: Regular HIPAA and security training

4.2 HIPAA Safeguards

We implement all required HIPAA safeguards:

  • Administrative Safeguards: Security management, workforce training, contingency planning
  • Physical Safeguards: Facility access controls, workstation security
  • Technical Safeguards: Access control, audit controls, integrity controls, transmission security

Information Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell your health information. We may share your information only in the following circumstances:

5.1 With Your Consent

We share information with healthcare providers, family members, or other parties only with your explicit written authorization.

5.2 For Treatment, Payment, and Healthcare Operations

We may share PHI with:

  • Healthcare providers involved in your treatment
  • Insurance companies for payment processing
  • Healthcare clearinghouses
  • Business associates under HIPAA agreements

5.3 Legal Requirements

We may disclose information when required by law, such as:

  • Court orders or subpoenas
  • Government investigations
  • Public health reporting requirements
  • Law enforcement requests (with proper legal process)

5.4 Service Providers

We may share information with trusted service providers who assist in operating our Service, subject to confidentiality agreements and HIPAA Business Associate Agreements where required.

Your Rights

🎯 You have important rights regarding your health information:

⚖️ HIPAA Rights

  • Right to access your health information
  • Right to request corrections
  • Right to an accounting of disclosures
  • Right to request restrictions
  • Right to confidential communications
  • Right to a copy of this notice

🔐 General Privacy Rights

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to rectification
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing
  • Right to withdraw consent

6.1 Exercising Your Rights

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Privacy Officer at privacy@meditrack.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days as required by HIPAA.

Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to:

  • Provide the Service to you
  • Comply with legal obligations (HIPAA requires retention for 6 years from creation or last use)
  • Resolve disputes and enforce agreements
  • Maintain business records

When we no longer need to retain your information, we will securely delete or anonymize it.

Children's Privacy

MediTrack+ is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

For minors between 13-18, we may process information with parental consent or as permitted by law for healthcare purposes.

International Data Transfers

If you are located outside the United States, please note that we transfer and process your information in the United States. We use appropriate safeguards, such as Standard Contractual Clauses, to protect your information during international transfers.
